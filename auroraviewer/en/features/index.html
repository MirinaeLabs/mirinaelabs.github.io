Product capabilities

More than a viewer:
choose and organize.

AuroraViewer connects browsing, comparison, ratings, tags, inspection, and export while preserving the source location of your files. These capabilities reflect the current source and version 1.6.3.

01Browse02Review03Compare04Inspect05Organize06Export

Browse

Bring sources into one library

Open individual images, multiple folders and their subfolders, plus images inside ZIP, RAR, and 7z as source groups. Finder opening and drag and drop are supported.

  • Add images, folders, and archives together
  • Collapse, expand, and reorder groups
  • Skip hidden items and duplicate URLs
Learn more

Keep the whole image and its detail in view

Move among Fit, Original Size, 200%, Preserve Scale, and pointer-centered zoom to examine composition and individual pixels.

Learn more

Search, sort, and narrow the library

Search names and extensions, then sort by capture or modified date, size, resolution, rating, and more. Filters can be combined.

Learn more

Review

Keep decisions beside the originals

Record 0–5 stars and Pick/Reject status, then read and write Finder tags. Apply them to a selection or the current filtered results.

  • Keyboard-first ratings
  • Create, apply, and remove Finder tags
  • Undo recent Aurora operations
Learn more

Compare

Place candidates side by side

Arrange images in two, three, or four slots and synchronize zoom and pan. For comics and scans, use two-page viewing with right-to-left ordering when needed.

  • Drag-and-drop slot placement
  • Independent or synchronized transforms
  • Animation playback and slideshows
Learn more

Inspect

Look closer at color and capture data

Magnify the pixel under the pointer and read RGB, HEX, and HSB values. Use the EXIF overlay for camera and capture information.

  • Color Picker and pixel preview
  • Histogram and channel views
  • Edit selected EXIF fields in JPEG, TIFF, and HEIC
Learn more

Organize

Group duplicate and similar images

Find exact duplicates by file hash, or group visually similar images with perceptual hashes and Apple Vision. Nothing is deleted automatically.

  • All, selection, or filtered-result scope
  • Adjustable similarity threshold
  • Review a suggested keeper in each group
Learn more

Export

Prepare files for the next task

Convert the current image, or copy and move selected originals. Choose crop, dimensions, quality, and metadata handling for the intended result.

  • PNG, JPEG, TIFF, HEIC, and WebP
  • Original size, 5–300% scaling, or explicit width and height
  • Keep, remove location, or strip metadata
Learn more
