One continuous workflow

Open, view, and organize
in one continuous flow.

You do not have to reopen files one at a time or move review decisions between tools. The AuroraViewer library keeps source location and working context together.

01 · OPEN

Open sources as they are

Add images, folders, nested folders, ZIP, RAR, and 7z; supported images are grouped by source.

02 · REVIEW

Choose the right view

Move from Fit to Original Size, 200%, two-page viewing, or two-to-four-slot comparison.

03 · ORGANIZE

Turn choices into results

Use ratings, Pick/Reject, Finder tags, file moves and copies, or converted export for the next step.

Library review

Narrow a large library
without losing your place.

Move between the list and thumbnail grid, search by file name or extension, and sort by capture date, resolution, rating, and more.

  • Ratings, Pick/Reject, extension, Finder tag, and animation filters
  • Batch ratings and tags for a selection or all filtered results
  • Undo for recent actions and background file operations
Explore organization features

Compare closely

A two-to-four-way view
for differences that matter.

Place candidates in slots and synchronize zoom and pan. Any slot can be made independent when you need a different area.

  • Two-, three-, and four-slot layouts with drag-and-drop placement
  • Per-slot or synchronized zoom and pan
  • Ratings, Pick/Reject, and Finder tags for the active slot

Broad input, careful output

Photos, RAW, documents,
and archives.

Inspect a file before converting it, then export the result with the format and dimensions the next task needs.

Images · RAW

JPEG, PNG, TIFF, HEIC, WebP, AVIF, PSD, and major camera RAW formats

Documents · Archives

SVG, AI, EPS, PDF, plus supported images inside ZIP, RAR, and 7z

Converted export

PNG · JPEG · TIFF · HEIC · WebP, with crop, resize, and metadata options

Availability and rendering quality can vary with the file's codec and the ImageIO or Quick Look support available in macOS.
